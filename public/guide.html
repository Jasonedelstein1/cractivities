01 Cahuita

~47 min from Dragonfly / ~56 min from Congo Bongo | 3 entries

Nature · Water

● Cahuita National Park — coastal trail Nature
Donation, $5–10 pp suggested · 2–4 h · No booking needed
Hours Daily 08:00–16:00 | Best Oct 7, Oct 10
Flat 8 km trail from Kelly Creek (Cahuita village) to Puerto Vargas along the beach and through jungle; the last stretch is a raised boardwalk.
  • Sloths, howler and capuchin monkeys
  • Raccoons that raid bags
  • Punta Cahuita white-sand beach halfway
Note: On a transit day, walk 45–60 min in and turn around. Park in the paid lot by Kelly Creek and leave nothing visible — bags will be in the car.
Booking None; guides at the Kelly Creek gate ~$25 pp
Pairs with Cahuita National Park — reef snorkel by boat; Cahuita village
○ Cahuita National Park — reef snorkel by boat Water
$45–55 pp, gear + fruit included · 2–2.5 h · Book ahead — Book 24 h ahead
Hours Daily 08:00–12:00 | Best Oct 7, Oct 10
Ten-minute boat to two reef spots inside the park's 600-hectare marine zone, then a fruit break on Punta Cahuita beach.
  • Costa Rica's largest Caribbean reef
  • Sea turtles, rays, nurse sharks on the sand, parrotfish
  • Sep–Oct is peak visibility
Note: Snorkeling here is guided-only. Conditions can cancel it the morning of — have the walk as plan B. Re-enter the park free after the boat. Tours at 08:15 and mid-morning.
Booking Required — guided only. Cahuita Experience meets 08:15 at their shop; Wildlife Tours CR $45 + $20 pp optional pickup from Manzanillo; Green Cahuita on GetYourGuide ($49). | W wildlifetourscr.com
Pairs with Cahuita National Park — coastal trail; Cahuita village
